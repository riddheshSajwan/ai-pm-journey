# **Opportunity Solution Tree** 

_distro.ai  ·  built from 3 user interviews (2 calls + 1 form response)_ 

**Outcome ->** 5 users visiting the platform at least once a week. 

### **Opportunities ->** 

**1.** _“Creating one good post takes half a day to a full day, because the research and then writing it up crisply is a lot of effort”_ **- pain** 

**2.** _“AI helps me summarise, but it hasn't cut down my time much, because what it produces still reads like slop and I have to fix that”_ **- pain** 

**3.** _“Commenting on other people's posts gets you reach, but a relevant comment takes real effort to analyse the post and write, and a generic one hurts more than helps”_ **- pain** 

**4.** _“Every post has to be approved before it goes out, and that slows everything down”_ **- pain** 

**5.** _“I want to find what is trending and turn it into a post fast, before the moment passes”_ **- desire** 

**6.** _“For technical content I have to wait on a technical person to write or proofread it, which costs me time and their time”_ **- pain** 

**7.** _“I want to know which of my posts worked and why, so I can do more of that”_ **- desire** 

**8.** _“I want to find and reach the right people to engage with, without hunting through LinkedIn manually”_ **- desire**

## **Top 3 opportunities** 

1. Drafting a good post takes too long (score 12) 

2. AI output reads like slop and still needs fixing (score 11) 

3. Making a relevant comment on others' posts is hard work (score 11) 

## **Easiest to build in 10 weeks: Opportunity 1 - drafting takes too long** 

It scored highest, every interview raised it, and unlike commenting it does not depend on reading other people's LinkedIn feeds, so there is no platform-rules problem to solve first. It is the cleanest thing to build and test. 

## **Solutions for Opportunity 1** 

**1.** A research agent that takes the topic you already chose and pulls the key facts, stats and recent angles into one short brief, so you skip the hours of hunting. 

**2.** A drafting agent that turns that brief plus your angle into a first post written in your saved voice, so you start from an editable draft, not a blank page. 

**3.** A crisp-it-up tool that takes your own rough writing and tightens it into a post-ready version, for when you want to write the substance yourself and only need the polish.
